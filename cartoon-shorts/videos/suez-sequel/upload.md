## Description
Part 2 of 2021's most famous traffic jam. The Ever Given got unstuck from the Suez Canal on 29 March 2021 - then it was held in the Great Bitter Lake for over three months while Egypt's Suez Canal Authority claimed about $916 million. After a settlement on 7 July 2021 it was released, and on 20 August 2021 it crossed the same canal again, escorted by two tugboats.
