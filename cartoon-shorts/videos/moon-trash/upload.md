Nobody lives on the Moon, so why not turn it into a landfill? Because landing just one kilo there costs over a million dollars. Weight is so expensive that the Apollo astronauts left their own trash behind to fly home lighter, including 96 bags of human waste.

## Fact notes (in case a commenter asks)
- "$1M+ per kilo": Astrobotic's published 2021 price is $1.2M per kg to the lunar surface ($4.5M per kg on a rover); prices differ between companies and years.
- "A week of your trash": EPA 2018 average of 4.9 lb per person per day = about 15.6 kg a week -> about $18.7M at $1.2M per kg (the number on the counter).
- "96 bags": the commonly cited count of human-waste bags left by the six Apollo landings (Royal Museums Greenwich); which bag holds what was never recorded, hence "some full of poop".
- "To fly home lighter": former NASA chief historian William Barry: "Weight was critical - we had to keep it down so we could get back off the surface of the moon."
