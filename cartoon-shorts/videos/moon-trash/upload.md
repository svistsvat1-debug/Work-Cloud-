$1M per kilo?   (cover.png - the frame-0 premise: the guy with his trash bag under the sleeping Moon)

## Description
Nobody lives on the Moon, so why not turn it into a landfill? Landing one kilo there costs over a million dollars, and that's exactly why the Apollo astronauts left 96 bags of their waste behind.

Sources:
- Astrobotic lunar payload price ($1.2M per kg, 2021 guide): https://science.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/astrobotic-lunar-landers-pug.pdf
- EPA, waste per person per day: https://www.epa.gov/facts-and-figures-about-materials-waste-and-recycling/national-overview-facts-and-figures-materials
- Royal Museums Greenwich, 96 bags of human waste on the Moon: https://www.rmg.co.uk/stories/space-astronomy/strange-things-humans-have-left-on-moon
- History.com, why Apollo crews left their waste (weight): https://www.history.com/articles/things-moon-astronauts-apollo
