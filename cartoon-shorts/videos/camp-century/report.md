## Сценарій (затверджений, слово в слово)
1. The US Army builds a town inside the Greenland ice, with a nuclear reactor.
2. Officially, for science.
3. Secretly, it's a test run for hiding six hundred nuclear missiles.
4. But the ice won't sit still.
5. It slowly squeezes the tunnels, so soldiers keep shaving the walls.
6. So the Army gives up and leaves.
7. Decades later, a NASA plane spots it by accident.
8. A hundred feet down.
