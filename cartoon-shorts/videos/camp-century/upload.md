**Description:**
The US Army built Camp Century inside the Greenland ice sheet in 1959, powered by a portable nuclear reactor. Officially it was a science base; secretly it tested Project Iceworm, a plan for up to 600 nuclear missiles under the ice - until the moving ice squeezed the tunnels. In April 2024 a NASA radar flight imaged the abandoned base by accident, at least 100 feet down.
