## 4. Озвучка
A lazy pigeon wants to land on your hand without flapping. Just glide in, right? But gliding needs speed, so it would
smack into you. Unless it swoops up from below, so the climb eats the speed. Engineers did the math. It needs over
twice its normal speed, a sixty-meter swoop and a twenty-meter climb. For one crumb. So it flaps. Right in your face.
