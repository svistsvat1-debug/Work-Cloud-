**Cover text:** ZERO SHARKS?  (cover.png)

**Description:**
A megalodon hiding in the Mariana Trench sounds like a movie plot, but sharks all but vanish below about 3,000 m: their big oily livers, which keep them afloat, need more rich food than the deep can give. (Megalodon itself died out about 3.6 million years ago.)
