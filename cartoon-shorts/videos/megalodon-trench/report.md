# megalodon-trench — фінальний звіт (відео #33, 2026-11-17)

## Сценарій (дослівно погоджений, 67 слів)
1. The last megalodon dives into the Mariana Trench to hide forever.
2. Nearly seven miles of darkness, who'd ever look?
3. But three miles down, there are no other sharks.
4. Not one.
5. Probably because a shark's giant oily liver, its float, takes lots of rich food, and only crumbs drift down here.
6. So the deeper it hides, the hungrier it gets.
7. At the bottom, dinner is two inches long.
