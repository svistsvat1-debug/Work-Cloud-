**Cover text:** 55X HARDER THAN MARS

**Description:**
Aiming a rocket of trash at the Sun sounds easy, but everything launched from Earth is already moving sideways at about 67,000 mph. Making it fall in takes about 55 times the energy of a trip to Mars, so leaving the solar system is actually easier.
