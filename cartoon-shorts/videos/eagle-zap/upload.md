**Title (chosen):** Birds Don't Get Zapped. So Why Do Eagles?

Other options:
- Why Do Eagles Get Zapped on Power Lines?
- What Happens If an Eagle Lands on Power Lines?

**Cover text:** 6-FOOT WINGS

**Description:**
Small birds can sit on one power line because the current has no path through them. An eagle's wings are wide enough to touch two wires at once, so the current goes through the eagle. That's why bird-safe poles keep wires about 60 inches (150 cm) apart.

Sources:
- APLIC (Avian Power Line Interaction Committee), Electrocutions: https://www.aplic.org/Electrocutions
- Loss, Will & Marra (2014), PLOS ONE, Refining Estimates of Bird Collision and Electrocution Mortality at Power Lines in the United States: https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0101565
