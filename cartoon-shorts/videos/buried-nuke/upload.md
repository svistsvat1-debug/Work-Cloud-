## Description
In January 1961 a B-52 broke apart over farmland near Goldsboro, North Carolina, and two hydrogen bombs fell. One came down on its parachute; the other hit a muddy field at about 700 mph and broke apart. Groundwater flooded the dig, and the part with the uranium was never recovered - in 1962 the landowners sold the government an easement: no digging below five feet.
