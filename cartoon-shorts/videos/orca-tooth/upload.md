**Description:**
Scientists counted 52 growth rings in the tooth of a male killer whale found on South Uist, Scotland, in 2015. But his oldest rings had no radiocarbon from the Cold War nuclear bomb tests, so he was born before that fallout reached the sea: his late-life rings had been squeezed too thin to count. He was likely about 65 or older, one of the oldest male killer whales on record.
