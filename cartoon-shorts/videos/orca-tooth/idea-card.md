## 4. Озвучка
Scientists count the rings in a killer whale's tooth, like a tree: fifty-two years. But nuclear bombs say they're wrong.
How? Cold War bomb tests sprinkled radioactive carbon into the ocean, so every ring since has it. His first ring doesn't.
So he was born before the fallout. The rings lied: his old-age ones were too thin to count. He was about sixty-five,
maybe more.
