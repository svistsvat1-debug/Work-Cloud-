# Звіт: titanic-cover (Short #36)

## Фінальний сценарій (слово в слово, як затверджено)
1. A Titanic hunter takes a secret Navy job: check two sunken nuclear submarines.
2. So the Titanic becomes his cover story.
3. But the subs come first.
4. Twelve days left.
5. Luckily, one sub taught him a trick: its pieces lie in a mile-long trail.
6. So he hunts the trail, not the ship.
7. At night, a boiler slides onto the screen.
8. The cover story comes true.
