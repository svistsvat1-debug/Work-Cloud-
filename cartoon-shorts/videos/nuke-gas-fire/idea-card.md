# Idea card (approved 2026-11-12) - nuke-gas-fire

Source: found by Claude (MODE B, proven demand + a fresh YouTube trend). Approved by the owner ("Ідея хороша робимо").

Idea: in 1963 a gas well in Uzbekistan blew out and burned for almost three years. In 1966 Soviet engineers set off a
nuclear charge about 1.5 km underground right beside it: the blast did not blow the flame out, it squeezed the rock and
pinched the well shut, like stepping on a hose. The flame went out 23 seconds later.

Voiceover (approved, 69 words, 375 characters):
> This fire burned for almost three years, until the Soviets nuked it. Why not just plug the well? They tried, but the gas, enough for a whole city every day, just escaped into nearby wells. So they set off a nuke almost a mile underground, right beside the well. The blast squeezed the rock and pinched the well shut, like stepping on a hose. Fire out in twenty-three seconds.

the well -> (because) the blast squeezed the rock and pinched the well shut, like stepping on a hose -> (so) fire out in
23 seconds -> loop: "...23 seconds" <-> "This fire burned for almost three years".
