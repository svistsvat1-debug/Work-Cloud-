**Title (pick):** How did nuclear tests catch a tree eating leftovers?
Other options:
- Why are maple leaves two years late?
- Why is a brand-new leaf made of old sugar?

**Cover text:** 2 YEARS LATE

**Description:**
Cold War bomb tests roughly doubled the radiocarbon in the air, and a maple-leaf record near Ottawa (picked since 1961) shows the leaves run about two years behind the air - because a sugar maple builds its new leaves roughly half from sugar it stored years earlier. The sugar in maple sap is, on average, three to five years old.

Sources:
- Wassenaar, Crann & Murseli (2026), "Stored carbohydrates buffer Bomb and Suess radiocarbon effects in maple leaves across a 65-year Ottawa region record", Scientific Reports 16: 37339 - https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-76700-9
- Muhr et al. (2016), "How fresh is maple syrup? Sugar maple trees mobilize carbon stored several years previously during early springtime sap-ascent", New Phytologist 209: 1410-1416 - https://www.newphytologist.org/news/how-fresh-is-your-maple-syrup
