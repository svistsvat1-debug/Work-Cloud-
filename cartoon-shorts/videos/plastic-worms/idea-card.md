# Idea card - plastic-worms (approved 2026-11-15: "Запускай виробництво")

Owner's theme: "how to get rid of trash" (short card asked for: idea + credits only). Idea: "Why don't we just feed our plastic to worms?" - you dump foam on mealworms, they really eat it, but 100 worms eat only ~35 mg (about a small pill) a day (Stanford 2015); the bacteria in their guts do the work. Credits planned: ~800 (cap 900).

Voiceover (written at approval, then frozen):
> So you dump a mountain of foam trash into a box of mealworms. And they actually eat it. They even breathe half of it out as air. But a hundred worms finish about one small pill of foam a day. So your mountain would outlast you, your kids, and the worms. Because the real eaters are tiny bacteria in their guts. So now scientists want the bacteria, not the worms.
