# Idea card - building-move (approved 2026-11-14)

Mode B (found by Claude) in the owner's niche "genius things people created". The owner approved the topic and asked for a
new rule: tell it as a short STORY (<= 20 s), not as a list of facts (rule saved in memory/STYLE.md). The story version of
the voiceover was written after that approval and went straight into production (the owner said "do it").

Idea: in 1930 Indiana Bell could not demolish its old Indianapolis building because it was a working phone exchange, so the
11,000-ton building was rolled and turned 90 degrees while about 600 employees kept working inside.

## Voiceover (final, story shape - 48 words, 268 characters)
In 1930, a phone company needed this building gone. The plan: knock it down. But it was a working phone exchange, so one
swing would cut off the calls. So the architect said: move it. They rolled and turned eleven thousand tons, with six
hundred people working inside.

(First, fact-shaped version in the card: "Why didn't they just knock this building down? In 1930, the phone company needed this
spot. But it was a working phone exchange, so tearing it down meant cutting off calls. So they rolled eleven thousand tons
aside and turned it, with six hundred people still working inside.")

Chain: 1930, the company wants the building gone -> so the plan: knock it down -> but it is a working phone exchange -> so
one swing cuts the calls -> so the architect: move it -> payoff: rolled and turned with 600 people inside.

## Packaging
Titles: **Why didn't they just knock this building down?** / Why did they move a building with people inside? / What if you
can't tear a building down? - Cover text: 600 PEOPLE INSIDE
