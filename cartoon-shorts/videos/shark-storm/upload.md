**Title (pick):** Why do sharks leave before a hurricane?
Other options: How do sharks know a storm is coming? / Why did 14 baby sharks flee their safe bay?

**Cover text:** BABY SHARKS KNEW  (cover.png)

**Description:**
Fourteen baby blacktip sharks with tracking tags left their shallow Florida bay hours before Tropical Storm Gabrielle hit in 2001, and came back weeks later. Scientists linked their exit to the falling air pressure ahead of the storm.
