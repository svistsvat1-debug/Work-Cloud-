## Description
In January 1960, Jacques Piccard and Don Walsh rode the bathyscaphe Trieste to the bottom of the Challenger Deep in the Mariana Trench: a steel crew ball hanging under a float full of gasoline. A window cracked on the way down, and the silt they stirred up at the bottom meant there are no photos.
