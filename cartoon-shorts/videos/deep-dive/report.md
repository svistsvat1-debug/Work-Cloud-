Two men sink toward the deepest spot on Earth, hanging under a tank of gasoline. Why gasoline? It's lighter than water, and unlike air, it barely squeezes. But five miles down, a bang shakes their steel ball. A window has cracked. They keep sinking anyway. After almost five hours, they land, kicking up mud that turns the window milky white. So from the bottom of the world... not one photo.
