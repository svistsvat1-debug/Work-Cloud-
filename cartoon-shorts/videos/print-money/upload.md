# print-money - upload kit

**Title (pick):** Why don't we just print more money?
Other options:
- What if a country just printed more money?
- Why can't a broke country print more cash?

**Cover text:** FOR 1 BREAD?

**Description:**
A country prints money to fix being broke, but the bakery still bakes the same bread, so prices climb. In Germany in 1923 a loaf ended up costing about 200 billion marks, and people burned banknotes in the stove because it was cheaper than buying firewood.

Sources:
- Hyperinflation in the Weimar Republic (loaf of bread in Berlin: ~160 marks at the end of 1922, ~200 billion marks by late 1923): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperinflation_in_the_Weimar_Republic
- McGraw-Hill economics reading "The German hyperinflation" (a housewife burned marks in her kitchen stove because it was cheaper than buying firewood with them; after Adam Smith, "Paper Money", 1981): https://highered.mheducation.com/sites/dl/free/0077151569/977708/c03f_The_German_hyperinflation.pdf
