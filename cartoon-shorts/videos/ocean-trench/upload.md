## Description (copy as is)
Why don't we just throw our trash into the deepest ocean trench and let the Earth swallow it? The seafloor there really does slide into the Earth, but only a few centimeters a year, about as fast as your fingernails grow. And some of what sinks can come back up through volcanoes.
