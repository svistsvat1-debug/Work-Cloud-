**Title (pick):** How did Easter Island's statues get their 12-ton hats?
Other options: How do you put a 12-ton hat on a stone giant? / Why didn't they just lift the Easter Island hats?

**Cover text:** JUST ROLL IT?  (cover.png: the stone giant wearing his red stone hat + the islander looking up + the text)

**Description:**
Some of the red stone hats on Easter Island's giants weigh up to 12 tons, and nobody had a crane. A 2018 study suggests the islanders rolled them up dirt ramps with a rope looped around them (parbuckling, which halves the pull) - and one hat still sits at the bottom of its ramp.
