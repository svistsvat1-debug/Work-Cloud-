## Voiceover (approved, used word for word)
Why didn't they just pour the Hoover Dam in one go? Concrete heats up as it hardens. In one piece, it'd take 125 years to cool, and crack. So they built it in blocks and pumped ice water through pipes inside. Basically, the dam got a fridge.

## Sources (U.S. Bureau of Reclamation)
- Concrete essay: a single continuous pour would have taken 125 years to cool; the stresses would have made the dam crack and crumble; built as blocks/columns with 1-inch cooling pipes (river water, then chilled water from a refrigeration plant on the lower cofferdam); pipes grouted afterwards.
- FAQ: 582+ miles of 1-inch pipe; the plant could make 1,000 tons of ice in 24 hours.
- Boulder Canyon Project history: cooling completed in 1935.
