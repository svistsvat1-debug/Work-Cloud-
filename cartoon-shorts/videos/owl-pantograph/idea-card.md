Source: the owner's idea from the chat (MODE A): "a story with an owl with a bombastic result". Next episode of #11 kingfisher-train (same engineer, same train, a different bird and a different problem).

## Voiceover (approved, used word for word)
Japan's new bullet train hit 300 kilometers an hour. But the arm on its roof howled in the wind, too loud for the homes nearby. So engineer Eiji Nakatsu, a birdwatcher, thought of owls. Tiny combs on their feathers chop the air, so they hunt almost silently. He copied the jagged edge, and the roof went quiet as an owl.

60 words, 326 characters, estimated ~23.5 s (real take: 22.16 s).

Story: doer = Japan's engineers / Nakatsu, wants the fastest train to run past homes; turn = the roof arm howls, too loud; ending = he copies the owl's jagged edge and the roof goes quiet.
Connector chain: train hits 300 -> but the arm howls -> so Nakatsu thinks of owls -> because combs chop the air -> so he copies the edge -> the roof goes quiet.
