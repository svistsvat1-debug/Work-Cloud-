# sahara-solar - idea card (2026-11-15)

The owner asked to make it at once without the approval stop ("Зроби зразу без жодного підтвердження"), so the card was not sent before production.

1. **Source:** MY IDEA (owner): "Why don't we just cover the Sahara in solar panels?" - MODE A.
2. **Why it should work:** the "why don't we just...?" shape that won on the channel (ocean-trench 25K, the only logged views); a simple obvious plan with a surprising chain of consequences. Not enough view data yet to compare story vs fact videos.
3. **Voiceover (69 words, 347 chars):**
   You want power for the whole planet, so you cover the Sahara in solar panels. Endless sun, right? But the panels are darker than sand, so the desert soaks up heat. The rising hot air pulls in rain, and the sand turns green. But that rain gets taken from the Amazon, so the jungle starts drying out. So the panels meant to cool the planet? They warm it up.
4. **Length:** estimate 69 / 3.06 = ~22.5 s; real take 20.96 s (Liam ~3.3 words/s), video 21.56 s.
5. **Story check:** doer = "you" (the guy), wants power for the whole planet; turn = "But the panels are darker than sand"; second turn = "But that rain gets taken from the Amazon"; ending = "They warm it up" (what finally happens, not a summary). Every sentence is an event; the order is causal (no two can swap); no names, dates or "discovered".
6. **Structure model:** the channel's own 'So you... But... So... But... So...' what-if story shape (volcano cut-out, STRUCTURES.md #1; no transcript). Hook 0-2 s ('you' + goal), turn at 6.2 s (29 %), second turn at 13.8 s (64 %), payoff in the last 1.5 s with a reversal (meant to cool -> warms). Niche mix: climate/energy what-if + storytelling shape + cut-out cartoon.
7. **Connector chain:** want power -> so cover the Sahara -> (so) endless sun -> But darker than sand -> so soaks up heat -> (so) hot air pulls in rain -> and (so) sand turns green -> But rain taken from the Amazon -> so jungle dries -> So panels meant to cool -> warm it up.
