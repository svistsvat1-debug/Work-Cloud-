**Title (pick):** Why don't we just cover the Sahara in solar panels?
Other options:

**Cover text:** IT GETS HOTTER?

**Description:**
Dark solar panels soak up more sunlight than pale sand, so a giant Sahara solar farm would heat the desert, pull in rain and turn it green. A climate model found that rain comes at a price: the Amazon gets drier and the whole planet warms a little.
