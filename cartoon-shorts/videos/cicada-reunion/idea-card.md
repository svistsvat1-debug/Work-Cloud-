A cicada in Illinois has waited underground for seventeen years. So it finally digs up into the sun... and bumps into a stranger. A cicada that only waited thirteen. Two different broods, coming up the same spring. So trillions of them sing at once, loud as a lawn mower. But the last time these two met, Thomas Jefferson was president. And the next time? Twenty-two forty-five.
