## Description
In 1966, an American hydrogen bomb fell into the sea off Palomares, Spain, after a mid-air collision. While the Navy searched for weeks, press officers spoke only of "the thing that is not a bomb". The tiny sub Alvin finally found it about half a mile down, and 80 days after the crash it was hauled up and shown to reporters.
