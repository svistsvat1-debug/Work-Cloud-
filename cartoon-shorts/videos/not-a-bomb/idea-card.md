## 4. Озвучка (англ., як історія)
A fisherman sees a man parachute into the sea. Except it's not a man. It's an American hydrogen bomb. So the Navy rushes in. But to reporters, it's "the thing that is not a bomb." For weeks, nothing. Until a tiny sub spots it, half a mile down. Eighty days in, it's finally up. And the thing that is not a bomb poses for the cameras.
