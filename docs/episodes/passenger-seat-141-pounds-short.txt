I was driving alone. But my truck weighed one hundred and forty-one pounds too much.

Three scales in three towns. The same number. To the pound.

And all of it was sitting on the front axle. Right under the passenger seat.

That seat had sunk all the way down. Like somebody had just sat in it.

Then an old scale ticket fell from behind my visor. Two weights in pencil. With June. Without June.

The difference was one hundred and forty-one pounds…
