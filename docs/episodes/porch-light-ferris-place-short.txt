That house has had no power since nineteen ninety-four. But every time my truck came over that hill at night, the porch light came on.

Only for trucks. Never for cars.

I counted. Two hundred and six times.

Then one Friday in April, at ten after one in the morning, the porch stayed dark.

And down in the hollow below the old road, something started flashing at me.

Two short. One long.

So I climbed over the guardrail…
