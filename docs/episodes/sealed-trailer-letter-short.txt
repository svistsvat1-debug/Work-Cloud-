The seal on my trailer was never broken. Number four four one seven.

Eleven hundred and forty miles. Nobody opened those doors.

North of Des Moines, I heard one hard knock. Right behind my head.

Twenty-eight below in Minnesota. The receiver cut the seal. I swung the doors open.

On top of the first pallet was an envelope. A fifteen cent stamp from nineteen seventy-nine. Never mailed.

And one word on the front, in blue ink.

Hank…

The full story is on my channel.
