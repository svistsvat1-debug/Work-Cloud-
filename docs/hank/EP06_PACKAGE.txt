Forty trucks on channel 19. One calm voice. And only my radio could hear it.
December 2001, an ice storm in the Missouri hills, and a stranger telling me exactly where to stop.

I was 24, hauling a light trailer and trying to get home to my boy for Christmas, when freezing rain turned Interstate 44 into glass. Then the CB went dead, and a voice I first heard in the fog back in 1998 told me to stop before the bridge at the bottom of the hill. Nobody else on the channel heard a word, and the next morning my dispatcher went quiet for a full minute before she said his name.
