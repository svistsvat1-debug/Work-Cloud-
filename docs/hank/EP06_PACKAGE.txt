=== DESCRIPTION · v2 ===
A trucker horror story from an ice storm in December 2001. Forty trucks on channel 19, one calm voice, and only my CB radio could hear it.
He told me exactly where to stop. The next morning, my dispatcher said his name.

I was 24, hauling a light trailer and trying to get home to my boy for Christmas, when freezing rain turned Interstate 44 into glass. Then the CB went dead, and a voice I first heard in the fog back in 1998 told me to stop before the bridge at the bottom of the hill. Nobody else on the channel heard a word, and my dispatcher went quiet for a full minute before she said who he was.
