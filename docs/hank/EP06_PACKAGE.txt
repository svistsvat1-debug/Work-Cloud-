=== DESCRIPTION ===
December 2001. An ice storm in the hills of Missouri. Forty trucks on channel 19, and only one radio heard him.
He told me exactly where to stop. The next morning, my dispatcher said his name.

I was 24, hauling a light trailer home for Christmas, when freezing rain turned Interstate 44 into glass. A calm voice I first heard in 1998 came on the CB and told me to stop before a bridge, and none of the other drivers could hear it. One phone call the next day gave that voice a name.
