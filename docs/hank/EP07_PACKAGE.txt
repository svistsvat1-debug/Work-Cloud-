=== DESCRIPTION ===
A trucker story about an old mechanic, one fake logbook page, and a promise I've kept every day for thirty years.
Ohio, 1996. My truck died on a hill. The old man who fixed it wouldn't take my last $31. He took my logbook instead.

I was nineteen, driving regional out of Columbus and chasing a fifty-dollar bonus, when I did something stupid: I drew eight hours of sleep in my logbook that I never had. The next morning, water in cheap diesel killed my engine on a back road south of Columbus. Virgil Tate walked down the hill from his little cinder block garage, fixed my truck for free, read every page of my log, and tore one out. Eleven years later his daughter handed me a cigar box, and I finally understood why an old man cared so much about a kid's logbook.
