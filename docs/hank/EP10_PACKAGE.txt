Every driver in that truck stop told him no. My dog wouldn't stop wagging his tail at him.

September 2008. I was 31, empty in Billings with a weekend to kill, and I'd never once broken the no-riders rule. Then an old man in a wool overcoat and a felt hat went from booth to booth asking for a ride up to the Hi-Line. He said he was trying to get home. The farther north we drove, the colder my cab got. At the end of an eleven-mile gravel road, there was a ranch house with half its roof gone, and a light in the kitchen window. He handed me an envelope, told me not to open it until he was inside, and walked up to the house.
