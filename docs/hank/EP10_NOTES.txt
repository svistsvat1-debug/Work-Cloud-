CONTINUITY NOTE (add to CANON):
- Ep 10: Saturday, September 13, 2008. Hank 31, long haul 10 years. Buddy has ridden with him almost 4 years.
  - Delivered steel pipe in Billings, Montana; no load until Monday; bobtailed (personal conveyance) about 500 miles round trip with a rider.
  - This is the only time in 31 years Hank broke the no-riders rule.
- Ellis Varner: born ~1937, a rancher's son on the Hi-Line (north of Billings, off Highway 2, gravel road 11 miles north past a grain elevator).
  - Feb 1956, age 18: fought with his father and walked out in a snowstorm with a brown cardboard suitcase tied with twine.
  - A westbound cattle truck picked him up and went off the road into a coulee. Ellis and the driver were found after the snowmelt. The suitcase was never found.
  - In 2008 he appeared as a man of ~70: wool overcoat, gray felt hat, cardboard suitcase. He knew the country, held the coffee but never drank it, and the cab got colder going north.
  - His line: "I've been trying to get home ever since."
- Tom (Thomas) Varner: Ellis's father. Withdrew $10,000 (everything he had) in Feb 1956 for search pilots and a reward; the money was never found. He kept the porch light on every night and died alone in 1971.
  - His check: "Pay to the order of the driver who brings my boy home. Ten thousand dollars. Thomas Varner. February 20, 1956. Memo: For Ellis." The bank closed in 1961.
- The ranch: abandoned since 1971, half the roof fallen in, a windmill with half its blades that creaks.
  - A kerosene-lamp light was in the kitchen window when they arrived, and went out after Ellis went in. The lamp was cold and full of dust.
  - Only Hank's and Buddy's footprints were in the dust. The cardboard suitcase was on a kitchen chair, covered in decades of dust.
- The envelope: $10,000 in old $100 bills with a paper bank band dated February 1956, plus Tom's check.
  - A young local deputy (his grandmother knew Ellis: "Eighteen years old, forever") told Hank: "I'd say you're the driver."
- What Hank did:
  - kept nothing for himself;
  - bought a granite headstone for Ellis (next to his father, in a small cemetery north of town; name, two years, "Home");
  - gave the rest to the county volunteer search and rescue;
  - keeps the check in his glove box, in every truck since.
- Present 2026: truck stop in Billings. Hank drives up the Hi-Line every year about this time and leaves a cup of coffee by the stone.
  - "Buddy's not with me anymore. That's a story for another night." This fits ep 29. Do not reveal how before ep 29.
