## Опис

```
On cold nights, many hummingbirds switch into torpor. They go still, their heart slows from about 1,200 to as low as 40 beats a minute, and they let their body cool down to save energy.

In the Peruvian Andes, one black metaltail cooled to 3.3 °C (38 °F). That is the lowest body temperature ever recorded in a bird.

Sources:
• Wolf et al. (2020), Biology Letters: https://royalsocietypublishing.org/rsbl/article/16/9/20200428/62818/Extreme-and-variable-torpor-among-high-elevation
• Cornell Lab of Ornithology, All About Birds: https://www.allaboutbirds.org/news/the-coolest-hummingbird-as-measured-by-an-actual-thermometer/
• Science News: https://www.sciencenews.org/article/hummingbirds-black-metaltail-cold-torpor
• Proceedings of the Royal Society B (2025), "The hummingbird's adipostat": https://royalsocietypublishing.org/rspb/article/292/2038/20242489/104787/The-hummingbird-s-adipostat-can-a-simple-rule

Animation and sound made in code. Voice-over generated with Kokoro TTS.

```
