**Опис:**
```
It should have fallen before it was even finished. Then the builders stopped for almost 100 years, and that pause let the clay under the tower squeeze out water and get stronger.

Sources: John Burland (Imperial College), "The stabilisation of the Leaning Tower of Pisa", Ingenia; Burland, Jamiolkowski & Viggiani (2009), Int. Journal of Geoengineering Case Histories.
```
