## Опис (копіювати як є)

```
A honey bee can sting another insect and usually fly away. Sting a human, and it dies.

Its stinger is barbed, so it gets stuck in your skin. When the bee pulls away, it tears out the stinger, the venom sac and part of its belly. The stinger keeps pumping venom for up to a minute, so get it out fast. It also releases a banana-scented alarm signal that calls more bees to the spot.

Only worker honey bees die this way. Wasps (and some other bees) can sting again and again.

Bee or wasp — which one's worse? 👇
```
