## Опис

```
Some viruses, called bacteriophages (phages), infect and kill bacteria but can't infect human cells. Doctors began treating patients with phages around 1919, years before the first antibiotics.

The catch: each phage only attacks certain bacteria, so every infection needs a matching phage. Antibiotics kill many kinds at once, so Western medicine mostly dropped phages. Now that more bacteria resist antibiotics, doctors are testing phage therapy again. It's still experimental in most countries.

Sources:
• Shukla & Aswani, Clinical Medicine & Research (2021) — early history of phage therapy in the US: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8231696/
• Adesanya et al., AIMS Microbiology (2020) — phages can't infect mammalian cells; host specificity: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7595837/
• Romero-Calle et al., Antibiotics (2019) — phages vs broad-spectrum antibiotics: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6784059/
• CIDRAP, University of Minnesota (2017) — a modern phage therapy case: https://www.cidrap.umn.edu/news-perspective/2017/11/save-life-doctors-turn-bacteria-killing-viruses

#science #biology #phages #antibiotics #microbiology #shorts
```
Перші три хештеги YouTube показує над назвою: #science #biology #phages.
