## Опис

```
Stand up too fast and your vision goes grey and sparkly? Here's what's happening.

When you stand, gravity pulls around half a liter to a liter of blood down into your legs. Your blood pressure dips for a moment, and less blood reaches your head. Your eyes notice first: you get sparks and a grey tunnel. Fighter pilots get the same grey tunnel in hard turns. It's called a grey-out.

Pressure sensors in your neck (baroreceptors) catch the drop. Your heart speeds up, your blood vessels tighten, and your vision comes back within seconds.

How often does this happen to you? Tell me in the comments 👇

⚠️ General information only, not medical advice. If it happens a lot, or you faint, talk to a doctor.

Sources:
```
