## 3 Thumbnail concepts (marker whiteboard, board #FAF8F3, max 3 objects, max 4 words)

### A ⭐ "The Convoy"
- **Left third:** a small soldier stick figure in a foxhole (olive fill, helmet), looking right.
- **Center → right:** ONE endless convoy of trucks snaking from the horizon toward him (a group = 1 object). A red marker arrow points along the road.
- **Top-right text:** `5,958 TRUCKS. ONE DAY.` in black Permanent Marker, red underline, yellow burst around "5,958".
- Objects: soldier, convoy, text = 3.
- Surprise not in the title: the **scale of one day's supply run**. The title asks "how many men?", and the thumbnail shocks with trucks.
- Pairs with **Title 1**.

### B "The Empty Rifle Slot"
- **Center:** a giant crowd block with the counter `8,000,000` (a group = 1 object).
- **Right:** an empty outline of a rifleman, dashed, with a red "?".
- **Text:** `NO RIFLEMEN LEFT?`
- Pairs with **Title 3**. Paradox on paradox. Risk: too abstract on mobile.

### C "Calories vs Shovel"
- **Center:** the soldier digging (shovel mid-swing). **Right:** a single K-ration box with a price tag style label `2,830 kcal`.
- **Text:** `NOT ENOUGH`.
- Pairs with **Title 5**. Weaker, because it only shows one quest.

Safety: no flags, no symbols, no blood in any concept. Germany is not on any thumbnail.

## Recommendation
**Title 1 + Thumbnail A.**
- Title = question (how many MEN?). Thumbnail = an unexpected answer about a different thing (TRUCKS, ONE DAY). That gives two separate shocks, and the hook gives the third ("a line of people no war movie shows").
- 3 objects, 4 words, readable at mobile size.
- Same visual world as the video: the convoy is a reused Scene 19/20 component.
