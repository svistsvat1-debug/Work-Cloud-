In 1944, one American rifleman walks toward the enemy across a field in Normandy. Behind him stands a line of people no war movie ever shows: farmers, factory workers, shipbuilders, merchant sailors, truck drivers, mail clerks, medics and surgeons.

How long was that line? We follow his breakfast, his bullets, his gasoline and his letters from home back across the Atlantic, through the Liberty ships, the Red Ball Express and V-Mail. Then we do the math with the U.S. Army's own numbers.
