## 2. Description
```
In August 1945, Japan still had thousands of hidden warplanes, a battered fleet and harbors full of mines. Two years later almost all of it was gone. So where did Japan's war machine actually go?

Fighters had their propellers pulled off. Aircraft carriers became ferries for more than six million stranded people. The surviving destroyers were divided among the Allies by drawing lots. The last Japanese battleship ended up under two atomic bombs at Bikini Atoll. And five years after the surrender, a few old Japanese minesweepers slipped out of port in secret, heading for a war in Korea.
```
