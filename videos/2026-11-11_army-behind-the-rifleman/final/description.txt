In Normandy, 1944, one American rifleman sat at the tip of the spear. But he could not last long on his own. Behind him stood an army he would never meet: cooks, truck drivers, mail clerks, medics and many more. So how many men did it take to keep one GI fighting?

We play it like a game: seven missions to keep one soldier in the fight, from K-rations and the Red Ball Express to the Six Triple Eight, the medics, the replacements and the enemy's own horse-drawn supply line. The answer comes at the end.
