For every American rifleman in Normandy, how many soldiers stood behind him? Cooks, truck drivers, mail clerks, medics, graves registration teams and the replacements who were never meant for the front. This is the hidden army that kept the WW2 GI fighting.

We play it like a game: seven missions to keep one soldier in the fight. K-rations, the Red Ball Express, Patton's fuel crisis, Victory Mail and the Six Triple Eight, the chain of evacuation, the riflemen shortage of 1944 and the German army's horse-drawn supply line. The real number comes at the end.
