## Description
It never gets knocked out… but is its brain REALLY fine? 🐦🧠

Part 2 of the woodpecker mystery 👉 Part 1: https://youtube.com/shorts/uc0ab1fAaQc

Woodpeckers slam their heads into trees all day without getting knocked out. But in 2018, scientists looked inside woodpecker brains kept in museums — and found clumps of tau, the same kind of protein buildup linked to brain damage in football players. Blackbirds, which don't peck, showed none. 😳

So is it damage… or protection? Even the scientists aren't sure yet. 🤔
