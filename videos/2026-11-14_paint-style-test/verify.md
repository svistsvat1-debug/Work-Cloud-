# [VERIFY] — test script facts

1. "In 1942 Britain is losing ships to German submarines faster than it can build new ones."
   Allied merchant losses in 1942 (~7.8 million gross tons, most to U-boats) were higher than new Allied construction that year
   (~7 million tons); new building overtook losses only in 1943. Strictly it is "the Allies", with Britain carrying most losses.
   Sources to check: S. W. Roskill, "The War at Sea" (HMSO), vol. 2, appendix on merchant shipping losses;
   https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_the_Atlantic (section "1942").
2. "Inventor Geoffrey Pyke pitches an aircraft carrier made of ice. Mixed with sawdust."
   Pyke (British, working for Combined Operations under Mountbatten) proposed the ice ship in 1942; the ice + wood-pulp/sawdust mix
   ("pykrete", about 14 % wood pulp) came from work by Herman Mark and Walter Hohenstein and was named after Pyke. "Mixed with sawdust"
   is a simplification of that order of events.
   Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Habakkuk ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pykrete
