## Option 1 — "Every WW2 Army's Food Ranked — Starving to Spoiled" ⭐ RECOMMENDED
- **Premise:** six armies' food ranked worst → best on 3 stated scores (on paper / taste / did it arrive); from Japanese soldiers eating grass to a US Navy barge that only made ice cream.
- **Formula:** FORMULA TRANSFER of The Rising Guy's card ("Every WW2 X Ranked — bad to good", 958K on a 9K-sub channel).
- **Swap axis:** hardware (weapons/vehicles) → **soldier life: food**, by country.
- **Sub-niche check** (`ytsearch30:"every ww2 ration ranked worst to best"` + `"ww2 rations of every army"`): **GREEN / YELLOW**
  - No video ranks WWII armies' food against each other (search returns only weapon rankings + single-country food videos).
  - Demand is proven and fresh, but on big channels: Tasting History "What Italian Soldiers Ate in World War II" 3.25M (4.52M subs, 2026-09-15); World War Wisdom "I Ate Nothing But WWII Rations For 3 Days" 1.03M (938K subs, 2026-03-25); Simple History "Worst MREs vs the Best MREs" 2.45M (2023).
  - Small channel: American Food History (28.5K subs) "25 FORGOTTEN U.S. Army Field Meals…" 93K (2026-07-25) ≈ 3x subs.
  - Honest read: demand for the *topic* is proven; demand for the *ranked-by-army* format on food is not yet proven by a small-channel outlier (it is proven on weapons).
- **Why people click:** ranking + "which army is #1?" + food is universal; a shock detail on the thumbnail (chocolate made to taste bad).
- **Why it's NOT a copy:** different subject (food, not hardware), our own criteria and through-line (starving → spoiled), Paint-style drawings instead of stock renders, a game structure with "but" bridges and human stakes.
