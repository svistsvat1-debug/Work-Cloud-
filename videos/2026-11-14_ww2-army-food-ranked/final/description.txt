Which army fed its soldiers best in World War Two? We rank six armies' food, from starving to spoiled, on three things: what the ration promised on paper, how it actually tasted, and whether it ever reached the soldier.

From Japanese soldiers on "Starvation Island" to a US Navy barge that made nothing but ice cream, this is the story of how food, trucks and factories decided who ate and who went hungry.
