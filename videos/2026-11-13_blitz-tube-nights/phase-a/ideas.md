## Option 1 — "How Londoners Ate, Slept & Survived 57 Nights Underground" ⭐ RECOMMENDED
- **Premise:** in the Blitz, the government banned sheltering in the Tube. Londoners bought a 1½d ticket and refused to leave. How 177,000 people a night ate, slept, went to the toilet and survived, and why the biggest danger underground turned out to be panic, not bombs.
- **Formula:** FORMULA TRANSFER.
- **Swap axis:** vehicle crews → **home front (civilians)**. Tank or bomber → a whole city sealed in tunnels.
- **Sub-niche check:** 🟡→🟢 **YELLOW-GREEN.**
  - Exact formula + subject: only 2 brand-new tiny uploads ("When Britain Was", "Ash and Ink History", 30–33 views). No channel owns it.
  - Demand for the subject is proven: Tasting History "Eating in a London Blitz Bomb Shelter" 693K (cooking format, big channel); Robslondon "What Was It Really Like To Shelter In The Tube During WWII?" 22.8K; Timeline "WW2 At Home" 489K.
  - Demand for the formula is proven: see diagnosis (Living on Alert 267×, Sealed In flak tower 480×).
  - Missing: a small-channel outlier on exactly this subject. Honestly, the subject is proven with big channels only.
- **Why people click:** the same "how did they eat/sleep/pee in there?" itch, plus a rule-breaking hook (government ban + 1½d loophole) and a city everyone knows.
- **Why it's NOT a copy:** different subject (civilians, not crews), different visual world (whiteboard, not AI-cinematic), and our own structure (6 quests plus the final twist: only 4% of Londoners used the Tube; Bethnal Green 1943).
- **Whiteboard fit:** excellent. Cross-sections, painted lines, bunks, food trains and numbers (177,000 / 27 miles / 7 tons / 4%).
- **Risk:** civilian subjects in this formula did less well than vehicles on Sealed In (Iwo Jima caves 9.5K). We offset this with a stronger hook (the ban) and a human face on the thumbnail.

## Option 2 — "How 338,000 Men Ate, Slept & Survived 9 Days on Dunkirk Beach"
- **Premise:** an army trapped on open sand with the sea behind it: water, food, sleep, toilets and the queue for the boats. [VERIFY numbers: 338,226 evacuated, 26 May–4 June 1940]
- **Formula:** FORMULA TRANSFER. **Swap axis:** vehicle crew → infantry stranded in the open.
- **Sub-niche check:** 🟡 **YELLOW.** No channel owns the formula + subject. Small POV videos sit at 1–1,083 views ("You're 1 of 400,000 Men Waiting to Die at Dunkirk", 1,083). The topic is big (Simple History "Dunkirk Evacuation" 2.9M), but there is no small-channel outlier.
- **Why click:** the famous movie, a famous number, and the "how did they wait 9 days?" question.
- **Not a copy:** new subject and angle; no "ate, slept & survived" Dunkirk exists.
- **Risk:** the "confined space" element is weaker (an open beach), which makes the claustrophobia hook less sharp.

## Option 3 — "Every Job in the London Blitz, Ranked From Safest to Deadliest"
- **Premise:** ARP warden, firefighter, bomb disposal, ambulance driver, Tube shelter marshal, factory worker… ranked by real risk.
- **Formula:** FORMULA TRANSFER of the second proven format ("Every Position Inside X Ranked From Safest to Deadliest": Inside the Steel King Tiger 1.2M; Behind the Armor U-boat 144K with 641 subs). **Swap axis:** vehicle positions → home-front jobs.
- **Sub-niche check:** 🟡 **YELLOW.** Only "What If You Had to Survive the Deadliest Jobs of the London Blitz?" (10 views). But the ranking format itself is now flooded with tiny AI channels at 3–1,800 views. Supply is overheating.
- **Why click:** rankings and "#1 isn't what you think".
- **Risk:** risk-per-job statistics are hard to source honestly. Many rankings would need [VERIFY] or would be opinion.
- **Bonus:** a natural follow-up to Option 1, since the shelter marshal appears in both.

## Recommendation
**Option 1.** It has the strongest proven formula, a free subject with proven demand, ideal whiteboard visuals (cross-sections + number cards), a clean safety profile (no gore needed) and a built-in sequel (the Anderson garden shelter, for the 27% who stayed home).
