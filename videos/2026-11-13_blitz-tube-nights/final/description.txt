In 1940, the government banned Londoners from sheltering in the Tube. So they bought the cheapest ticket, walked down to the platform and refused to leave.

For 57 nights in a row, German bombers came back to London. Up to 177,000 people a night slept underground. How did they eat, sleep, find a toilet and survive? And why did the biggest danger underground turn out to be something other than the bombs?
