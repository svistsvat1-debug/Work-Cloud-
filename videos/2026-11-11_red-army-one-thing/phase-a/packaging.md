## Thumbnail tactic transfer
Ink Story's tactic: **one big cartoon soldier with an exaggerated emotion + 1–3 yellow words that break expectations**.
Our version: one big soldier drawn in marker (plain olive pilotka cap with **no star**), wide-eyed and puzzled. The text is short and contradicts what the viewer expects of an army. Marker on the white board, with a yellow burst and a red underline.
