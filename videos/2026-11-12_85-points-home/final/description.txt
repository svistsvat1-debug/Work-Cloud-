The war in Europe ended on May 8, 1945. Millions of American soldiers expected to go home. Instead, the Army handed them a number: 85.

In this animated WW2 documentary, you play the game they played. Count the points of three typical soldiers (a father, a young combat veteran and a teenage replacement) and see who really made it home, who was lined up for the invasion of Japan, and why winning the war ended with soldiers protesting under the slogan "No boats, no votes".
