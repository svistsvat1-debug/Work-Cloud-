## Description
Why would one man pour water on the sand while over a hundred others drag a giant statue? 🏺🤔

This scene is painted in the tomb of Djehutihotep in Egypt — almost 4,000 years old. For a long time the water was read as a ritual. Then in 2014, physicists at the University of Amsterdam tested it with a model sledge on sand. Watch to the end to see what the water actually does 👀

Careful with the headlines that say this "solved the pyramids": the painting shows a statue, not a pyramid block, and archaeologists think the Egyptians used several methods (wet clay tracks too). Whether the painter meant ritual or physics is still debated.
