## Voiceover (approved, 49 words)
Workers keep adding steps under a golden angel in Mexico City. Fourteen new steps... but the column never grows. Why? The city around it is sinking. From space, a radar sees parts of it drop half an inch a month. Every year, the angel rises... without moving at all.

## Facts
- NASA JPL 29 Apr 2026: "Fourteen steps have been added to its base as the surrounding land has sunk"; NISAR data 25 Oct 2025 - 17 Jan 2026: areas sinking more than half an inch (2 cm) per month; main cause groundwater pumping.
- Wikipedia (citing the Mexico City government site): originally nine steps, fourteen added. One blog / Inkl say it had no steps at first - not used in the script.
- Source video's ".5-.8 inches a month" and "10 inches a year" not used.
