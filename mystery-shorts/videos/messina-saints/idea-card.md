## Voiceover (approved, 51 words)
Two painted saints lean on a wall in Sicily, alone at night. Minutes earlier, masked thieves break into the city museum. They take Antonello da Messina's paintings. But outside, they drop two panels... and vanish. Experts say the rest can't be sold. So why steal them... and leave two saints behind?

## Facts (verified)
- 15 Aug 2026 (Ferragosto), ~9:50 pm, Regional Museum of Messina (MuMe); at least three masked people; railing bent/cut, exterior door forced; went straight to the Antonello rooms; a few minutes (Time, Finestre sull'Arte).
- Taken: panels of the San Gregorio Polyptych (1473) + a double-sided panel; two side panels (Saints Gregory and Benedict) abandoned on a low wall outside, found by a passer-by (Il Sole 24 Ore, Time).
- Experts: works unsellable because instantly recognizable; ~70-80 M EUR estimate (unofficial).
- Status checked 2026-11-16: no reports of arrests or of the rest being recovered; "paintings recovered, 9 Moldovans" reports are about the March 2026 Parma theft, not Messina.
