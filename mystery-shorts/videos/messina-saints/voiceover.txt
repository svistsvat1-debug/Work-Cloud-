Two painted saints lean on a low wall in Sicily, alone in the dark. Earlier, while the city watches a holiday procession, masked figures bend the museum railing and force a door. They go straight for Antonello da Messina's paintings. In minutes, the panels are gone. The guards notice nothing. But outside, the thieves drop two panels on a wall... and vanish. A passer-by finds them and calls the police. Experts say the rest are too famous to ever sell. So who steals paintings no one can buy... and leaves two saints behind?
